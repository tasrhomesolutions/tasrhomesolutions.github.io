Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TASR Home Solutions LLC ("TASR," "we," "us" or "our") collects, uses and protects information when you visit tasrhomesolutions.com, send us a service request, or call, text, WhatsApp or email us.

Text messaging in short: We use your phone number only to text you about your service requests and appointments. We do not sell or share your mobile number or text message consent with anyone for marketing. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help.

Information we collect

Information you give us

When you send a service request or contact us, we may collect:

  • your name, phone number, email address, ZIP code and service address;
  • details about your appliance and the problem, such as brand, model, error codes and symptoms;
  • photos or videos you choose to send us;
  • whether you agreed to receive text messages, and when;
  • anything else you tell us in calls, texts, emails or messages.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, the companies that host and serve our website may automatically log basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device type and the pages you view. We do not use advertising cookies or sell browsing data.

How we use your information

  • to respond to your request, schedule a visit and confirm your arrival window;
  • to diagnose, quote, repair or install your appliance;
  • to send appointment reminders, updates and service follow-ups;
  • to send invoices and receipts and to handle questions about our work;
  • to keep records required for our business and to meet legal obligations.

Text messages (SMS)

If you check the text message consent box on our booking form, or otherwise ask us to text you, TASR Home Solutions LLC will send appointment and service text messages to the phone number you provide. These include appointment confirmations, arrival updates, reminders, quotes and follow-ups about your service.

  • Message frequency varies based on your service.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out. We will send one message confirming you have been unsubscribed and no further texts after that.
  • Reply HELP for help, or call 781.708.2257.
  • Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any service.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service or the text messaging platform that delivers our messages, is permitted. All other categories of sharing described in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent.

How we share information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers who help us run the business, such as our website host, the service that delivers booking-form requests to our email, our email and phone providers, and our text messaging platform. They may use the information only to provide their services to us.
  • With parts suppliers or manufacturers, limited to what is needed to order parts or handle a warranty claim for your appliance.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our customers, our technicians or the public.
  • In a business transfer, if TASR is sold or merged, in which case this policy continues to apply to your information.

How long we keep information

We keep customer and service records for as long as needed to provide service, honor any warranty, keep required business and tax records, and resolve disputes. Photos you send are kept only as long as they are useful for your service.

How we protect information

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect your information. Our website uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

  • Stop text messages at any time by replying STOP.
  • Ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us below. We may need to keep some records where the law requires it.

Children

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Other websites

Our website links to other services, such as Google reviews and WhatsApp. Their own privacy policies apply when you use them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.
